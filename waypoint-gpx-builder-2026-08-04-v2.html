Step 3 — Describe each waypoint

Type must be one of the app's fixed categories — pick from the list, don't type your own. Label is optional (an admin-only note, not shown to walkers). Elevation isn't needed here — Explore Crete fills that in automatically when you import.

Primary-Start is where a new commentary location begins — this is the only kind of point walkers actually see in the app. Primary-Stop marks where that location ends (admin/narrator only — used to work out how much audio time is available, never shown to walkers). Secondary is any other trigger point along the way (admin/narrator only, but its audio still plays for walkers when they pass it). Elevation isn't needed here — Explore Crete fills that in automatically when you import.
